# Category 15 — Text substitution and manipulation

Adapted from [w3resource: Text substitution and manipulation](https://www.w3resource.com/bash-script-exercises/text-substitution-and-manipulation.php).
Every exercise edits `temp.txt` in place; the fixture also leaves an
untouched `temp_orig.txt` backup that checkers use to recompute the
expected result live.

### c15-01 — Replace a word

Your script takes two arguments, an old word and a new word. The
exercise comes with `temp.txt`. Replace every occurrence of the old word
with the new one, in place.

### c15-02 — Uppercase conversion

Convert all of `temp.txt`'s contents to uppercase, in place.

### c15-03 — Lowercase conversion

Convert all of `temp.txt`'s contents to lowercase, in place.

### c15-04 — Reverse line order

Reverse the *order* of the lines in `temp.txt` (last line first), in
place — not the characters within each line.

### c15-05 — Reverse characters in each line

Reverse the *characters* within each line of `temp.txt` (the line order
stays the same), in place.

### c15-06 — Remove empty lines

The exercise comes with `temp.txt`, which has a blank line in it. Remove
all blank lines, in place.

### c15-07 — Trim leading and trailing whitespace

The exercise comes with `temp.txt`, whose lines have extra leading and
trailing spaces. Trim the whitespace from the start and end of each
line (internal spacing between words can stay as-is), in place.

### c15-08 — Insert text at the start of each line

Prefix every line of `temp.txt` with exactly `New Text - `, in place.

### c15-09 — Insert text at the end of each line

Suffix every line of `temp.txt` with exactly ` Text inserted at the
end`, in place.
